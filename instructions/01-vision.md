# CareerPilot — Vision & Mission

## What It Is

CareerPilot is an AI-powered career development platform built for the UAE job market.
It is owned and operated by **AxisMind**, founded by Ayoub.

The platform helps job seekers:
- Build ATS-optimized resumes and cover letters
- Prepare for interviews with AI coaching
- Discover career paths and skill gaps
- Automate job applications at scale

## Target Market

**Primary:** UAE job seekers — recent graduates, professionals switching roles, expats entering the UAE market.

**Secondary:** Hiring managers and companies who want to connect with pre-screened, AI-coached candidates.

**Geography:** UAE-first (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah). Arabic + English bilingual long-term.

## Mission Statement

> Help users discover career paths, build roadmaps, acquire skills, track progress, and connect with opportunities through AI-powered guidance.

## Core Differentiators

1. **UAE-specific** — templates, tone, and job market guidance tailored for the UAE (not generic Western platforms)
2. **All-in-one** — resume + cover letter + interview prep + automation in one subscription
3. **Affordable** — priced below competitors to capture UAE market share at launch
4. **AI-native** — every step of the career journey has AI assistance baked in
5. **Automation** — the only platform offering automated job application outreach as a standalone feature

## Brand Positioning

Premium but accessible. Modern, clean, youth-friendly. Feels like a career co-pilot sitting next to you — not a cold enterprise tool.

## Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| One-time bundles | Starter Bundle (10 AED), Pro Bundle (39 AED/mo) |
| Annual subscription | Pro Annual (299 AED/yr) |
| Automation plan | 29 AED/mo standalone |
| Growth bundle | Pro + Automation (59 AED/mo) |
| Coupon system | Psychological anchoring — e.g. WELCOME50 makes Starter 5 AED |

## Competitors (UAE)

- Bayt.com — job board, no AI coaching
- Naukrigulf — job board, no resume builder
- Resume.io / Zety — generic, not UAE-specific, expensive in USD
- LinkedIn Premium — expensive, not resume-focused

CareerPilot undercuts all of them on price and goes deeper on AI-powered career coaching.
